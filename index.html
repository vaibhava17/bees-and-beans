    From block to box

    01/ 05

    1. Step 01 · The block

      It starts with one kilogram.

      Every batch begins with 1 KG blocks of authentic chocolate — a base we trust for its flavour, gloss and clean snap.

    2. Step 02 · Broken by hand

      Broken along the grooves.

      Each block is broken into pieces by hand, so it melts gently and evenly — never scorched, never rushed.

    3. Step 03 · Melted slowly

      Melted into a glossy pour.

      A little at a time, the pieces become smooth, shining chocolate — ready for moulds, dipping and piping.

    4. Step 04 · Shaped & finished

      Turned into something personal.

      Truffles, rochers, mendiants and hearts — moulded, piped and topped with roasted nuts, berries and seeds.

    5. Step 05 · Packed for the occasion

      Boxed, ribboned, ready to give.

      Laid into our gift boxes and tied with the Bees & Beans ribbon — for Diwali, Christmas, the New Year or your whole team.
